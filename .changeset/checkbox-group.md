Add Checkbox Group (`lui-checkbox-group` and the CSS-only `.checkbox-group`) to group checkboxes that answer one question, with a legend, hint, a single group-level error and `required` meaning at least one option checked. Each checked option submits its value under the group `name`. Inside a group, `lui-checkbox` no longer renders its own error message
