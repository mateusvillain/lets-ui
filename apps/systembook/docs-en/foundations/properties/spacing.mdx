## Purpose

Space is the cheapest way to organize a screen. Before a border, a background color or a heading, the **distance** between two elements already says whether they belong to the same group.

A closed scale ensures this language is the same across the whole product: the space between the label and the field is always the same, and the eye learns to read the pattern.

## Principles

- **Proximity groups.** Items in the same group are closer to each other than to any other group.
- **Rhythm comes from repetition.** Few values, always used the same way, create rhythm. Many similar values create noise.
- **White space is content.** It gives focus, separates decisions and lets the screen breathe.
- **Density is a choice.** Screens for intensive work call for less space; reading and decision screens call for more. The choice is per screen, not per element.
- **Only values from the scale.** If the scale does not have the value you want, the problem is probably something else.

## Density, readability and rhythm

The three forces pull in different directions, and space is where they balance:

| Force | Asks for | Risk when overdone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Density** | Less space, more information per screen. | Groups blur together and reading gets tiring. |
| **Readability** | More space around text and between blocks. | The person scrolls too much and loses context. |
| **Rhythm** | The same intervals, repeated. | A monotonous screen, without hierarchy. |

A practical rule: **double** the space at each level of grouping. If list items are at `8`, groups are at `16` and sections at `32`.

## The two scales

| Scale | Values | Behavior | SCSS function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed** | `0`, `8`, `16`, `24`, `32`, `40` | Absolute value in pixels, on a base of 8. | `fixed($n)` |
| **Fluid** | `2`, `4`, `8`, `12`, `16`, `20`, `24`, `32`, `40`, `48`, `56`, `64`, `72`, `80` | `clamp()` between 90% and 100% of the nominal value, in `rem`. | `fluid($n)` |

The token number is the **nominal value in pixels**. `fluid(16)` is "the 16 of the fluid scale" and `fixed(16)` is "exactly 16px".

### When to use each one

| Use | When | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fluid** | It is the default scale for component and layout padding and gap. It is finer (14 steps) and follows the person's font size, because it is written in `rem`. | Button padding, Stack gap, Card padding. |
| **Fixed** | The value must be exact, or it is a structural measure that must not give way. | Icon Button size (40px), [Grid](grid.mdx) gap and margin, Tooltip positioning distances. |

<Callout variant="warning">
  **Known limitation.** The `vw` coefficient of the fluid tokens is too small to take the value from minimum to maximum within real screen widths. In practice, today every fluid token resolves to the **minimum** (90% of the nominal) at any viewport: `fluid(16)` is 14.4px on both mobile and desktop. The behavior is stable and predictable, but not fluid yet. The minimum and maximum values in the table still stand as the contract.
</Callout>

## Rules by context

Let's UI has no composition tokens (such as `padding.component.md`): the context is decided by the components and the layout primitives, always with values from the scale. The table below is the reference for what you build.

| Context | Range | What the components use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inside a control** (icon and text, prefix and value) | `fluid(4)` to `fluid(8)` | Button and Dropdown Menu gap: `fluid(8)`. Tag gap: `fluid(4)`. |
| **Control padding** | `fluid(8)` to `fluid(16)` | Button `md`: `fluid(8)` × `fluid(12)`. Button `lg`: `fluid(12)` × `fluid(16)`. |
| **Surface padding** | `fluid(16)` to `fluid(24)` | Card, Alert and Toast: `fluid(16)`. Modal: `fluid(24)`. |
| **Between related elements** (title and text, label and field) | `fluid(8)` | Card body: `fluid(8)`. |
| **Between groups** (a card's actions, fields from different sections) | `fluid(16)` to `fluid(24)` | Card and Modal actions: `fluid(16)`. Default Stack: `fluid(16)`. |
| **Between page sections** | `fluid(32)` to `fluid(80)` | Use a Stack with gap `32` or more. |
| **Layout** (page margin, gutter) | Fixed scale | Grid margin: 16 / 32 / 40px. Grid gap: 16 / 24px. Container padding: `fluid(32)`. |

## How to apply

The preferred way is to let the **container** control the space, with `gap`, instead of loose margins on the children.

| Tool | For | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Stack](../../components/layout/stack/index.mdx) | Items stacked vertically. | `<lui-stack gap="24">` or `.stack--gap-24` |
| [Inline](../../components/layout/inline/index.mdx) | Items in a row, which wrap. | `<lui-inline gap="8">` or `.inline--gap-8` |
| [Grid](../../components/layout/grid/index.mdx) | Page structure in columns. | Gap and margin come from the brand tokens. |
| Utility classes | One-off adjustments in HTML. | `.gap-fluid-16`, `.p--fluid-24`, `.m-t-fixed-16` |
| SCSS functions | Components. | `padding: fluid(16);` |

```scss
.summary {
  display: flex;
  flex-direction: column;
  gap: fluid(8);
  padding: fluid(24);
}
```

## Examples

### Card

`fluid(16)` padding on the body, `fluid(8)` between title and text, and `fluid(16)` before and between the actions. The jump from 8 to 16 separates the content from the actions.

### Form

`fluid(8)` between the label, the field and the help message; `fluid(16)` to `fluid(24)` between fields; `fluid(32)` between groups of fields with their own heading; `fluid(24)` between the last field and the buttons.

### List

`fluid(8)` to `fluid(12)` between simple items; `fluid(16)` between items with a title and description. In dense lists, use [Divider](../../components/layout/divider/index.mdx) instead of more space.

### Modal

`fluid(24)` of padding at the edges, `fluid(8)` between the header and the body, and `fluid(16)` between the footer buttons.

### Page

Side margin and gutter come from the [Grid](grid.mdx). Between sections, `fluid(48)` to `fluid(80)`, with the largest value separating the top of the page from the rest.

## Accessibility

- **Touch target.** Every interactive control has at least **24 × 24px** of area (WCAG 2.5.8), or enough free space around it to complete 24px. The system's main controls use 40px.
- **Space between targets.** Two small controls side by side need enough distance not to be triggered by mistake. Prefer `fluid(8)` or more.
- **Zoom and enlarged text.** The fluid scale is written in `rem` and grows with the font size the person chooses. Test with text enlarged to 200%: nothing can overlap or be cut off.
- **Text spacing.** The layout must withstand the increase in line height and letter and word spacing the person applies (WCAG 1.4.12). Do not fix heights on text containers.

## Best practices

- Use the layout primitives to apply space. They already use the scale.
- Prefer `gap` on the container to `margin` on the children: the space lives in one place, and nothing is left over after the last item.
- Double the space at each level of grouping.
- On dense screens, reduce the space **uniformly**, one step per context, instead of squeezing just one place.
- Use the fixed scale for structural measures and the fluid one for the rest.

## Anti-patterns

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Space proportional to the relationship">
  Label right next to the field (`fluid(8)`), fields `fluid(16)` apart, sections `fluid(32)` apart. The person reads the groups without any border.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Values outside the scale">
  `margin-top: 13px` here and `22px` there. The screen loses its rhythm, and the next adjustment becomes a guess.
</DosDonts>

- **The same space for everything.** If everything is at 16px, nothing is grouped.
- **Margin to "fix" alignment.** A `margin-left: 3px` to align an icon hides a structural problem. Fix the container.
- **Adding tokens to reach a value.** `calc(fluid(16) + fluid(4))` is an arbitrary value under another name.
- **Fixed height on text blocks.** Text grows with zoom, translation and user spacing; the box does not.
