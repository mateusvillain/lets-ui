## Where centering makes sense

- Page-level **empty state**, **loading** and **error** screens.
- **Highlight sections** with a heading and a button.
- **Standalone** elements, such as a logo.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Short centered blocks">
  A heading, a sentence and a button in the center of the screen. Attention goes to the only content.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Long centered paragraphs">
  Centered text has ragged line starts and is hard to read. Align it to the left.
</DosDonts>

## Height and width

- Set `min-height` when you want to center vertically within an area, such as `100vh` on a full page.
- Use `max-width` so the content does not stretch on wide screens.

## Best practices

- Center **few elements** at a time.
- Combine with [Stack](../stack/index.mdx) to arrange the centered content in a column.
- Avoid centering long forms: align them to the left.
