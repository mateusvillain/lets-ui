## Use sparingly

White space, grouping and typography separate content better than lines. Use Divider when **space alone is not enough**.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Separating groups with different meanings">
  In a menu, a Divider before the destructive action. The line signals that it is different from the others.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="A line between every item">
  Lines between all the items of a list. The screen feels heavy, and the hierarchy disappears.
</DosDonts>

## Divider with text

Use the text to mark a **choice between alternatives** ("or") or to **name a group**. Keep the word short.

## Vertical

Use it vertically to separate items in an action bar. Make the Divider as tall as the neighboring items.

## Best practices

- Keep the same separator style across the whole product.
- Do not use Divider as the only way to indicate grouping: spacing also needs to group.
- In menus, do not place Dividers back to back, or at the start or end.
