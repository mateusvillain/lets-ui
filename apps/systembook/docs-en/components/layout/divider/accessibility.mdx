---
title: "Accessibility"
---

- Divider is a **visual** separator. In contexts such as menus, it is announced as a separator; on pages, screen readers ignore it.
- Do not use Divider as the only way to convey that two pieces of content are different. Also use **headings** and **structure**.
- The line must have a contrast of at least 3:1 against the background when it conveys information. When it is decorative, it can be subtler.
- In a divider with text, the text needs a contrast of 4.5:1.
