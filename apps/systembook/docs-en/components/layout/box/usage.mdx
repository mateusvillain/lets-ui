## Choose the background by meaning

- `neutral/surface` and `neutral/container` for common areas.
- `primary` and `secondary` for brand highlights.
- `info`, `success`, `caution` and `danger` for content with a **state**, such as a notice at the bottom of a form.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Tokens, not one-off colors">
  Use `background="neutral/surface"`. The color follows the dark theme and the brands with no rework.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Loose color and spacing values">
  Typing a hex value or a spacing outside the scale. The Box stops following theme and brand.
</DosDonts>

## Spacing

Use `padding` from the scale: **16** for compact blocks, **24** or **32** for highlight areas. Keep the same spacing in blocks of the same kind.

## Border and radius

- Prefer **background or border**, not both strong at the same time.
- Use the `md` radius for content blocks and `lg` for larger areas, staying consistent with Cards.

## Best practices

- Do not nest many Boxes. If the content needs several layers, rethink the hierarchy.
- Use Box to **group**, not to decorate.
- Ensure text contrast on the chosen background (4.5:1).
