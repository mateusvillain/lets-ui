## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Side area and main area">
  Side navigation and content, filters and results, list and detail.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Fixed-width side area">
  The side area keeps its width, and the content takes the rest. When space runs out, the two areas **stack**.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="A multi-column grid">
  Use [Grid](../grid/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Panels that slide over the page">
  Use [Drawer](../../content/drawer/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Two columns of equal weight">
  Use [Switcher](../switcher/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

## Options

| Option | What it controls | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `side` | Which side the side area sits on: `start` or `end`. | `start` |
| `side-width` | Width of the side area. | 240px |
| `content-min-width` | Minimum content width before stacking. | 50% |
| `gap` | Space between the areas. | 24 |
| `align` | Vertical alignment. | Stretch |
