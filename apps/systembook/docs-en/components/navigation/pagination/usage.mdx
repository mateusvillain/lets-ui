title: "Usage"
---

## Position

Place the Pagination **below** the list or table, aligned with the content. In very long lists, repeat it above as well to avoid unnecessary scrolling.

## Give context

The person needs to know **how many items there are**. Pair the pagination with text like "Showing 21 to 40 of 312 results" and, when it makes sense, with an items-per-page control.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Total and position visible">
  "21–40 of 312 results" next to the pagination. The person understands the size of the set and where they are.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Paginating without context">
  Only the numbers, with no total and no sign of what changed when switching pages. The person feels lost.
</DosDonts>

## Behavior when changing pages

- **Go back to the top of the list** when changing pages.
- Keep **filters and sorting** when moving between pages.
- Update the URL with the current page, so it can be shared and reached again with the browser's back button.
- If the list becomes empty after a deletion, go to the previous page.

## Number of items

- Choose a page size that balances performance and readability, usually between 10 and 50 items.
- Avoid fewer than 10 items per page, as it forces the person to click too much.

## Best practices

- Do not show the pagination when there is only one page.
- Keep the previous and next buttons in the same place at all times.
- Customize the accessible texts in the product's language ("Previous page", "Next page").
