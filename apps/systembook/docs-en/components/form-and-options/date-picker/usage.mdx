## Calendar or typing?

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Upcoming date, or the weekday matters | Calendar (default) |
| Distant date the person already knows | `editable` |
| Period, such as a stay | `range` |
| Period the person already knows, such as contract dates | `editable range` |

With `editable`, the person can still open the calendar. The reverse does not hold: without `editable`, typing is not possible, and getting to 1985 month by month is tiring, even with the month and year grids.

## Limit the dates

- Use `min` and `max` to cut off the ends: a booking that cannot be in the past, a deadline that cannot go past the end of the quarter.
- For gaps in the middle, such as holidays or days already booked, mark the days as **unavailable**. They get a strikethrough, not just a fainter color, so they are not confused with days outside the limit.
- In a period, only the ends need to be available: a stay can span an unavailable day.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Limits visible before the error">
  A check-in that only accepts dates from today onward: past days cannot even be chosen.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Accept and reject later">
  Letting people choose any date and only saying, on submit, that it cannot be in the past.
</DosDonts>

## Language and week

- Set the product's `locale`. It changes the language of the month and the weekdays and the field's format, but not the submitted value, which is always in ISO 8601 (`2026-01-16`).
- The field writes the month abbreviated: "Jan 16, 2026". `10/11/2026` is October in the United States and November in Brazil; the written month avoids the confusion.
- Set the first day of the week according to the audience: Sunday in Brazil and the United States, Monday in most of Europe.

## Label, hint and errors

- The label says **which date**: "Due date", "Check-in". With `range`, name the period: "Stay".
- With `editable`, the format appears below the field. If you use a hint, include the format in it.
- Write a message for each error, saying what to do: "Choose a date from today onward", "The end date can't be before the start date".

## Best practices

- Do not use three Selects (day, month, year) when the Date Picker does the job.
- Do not prefill with today's date if the person really needs to choose: many people submit without noticing.
- For periods, show the total beside it when it matters, such as "5 nights".
