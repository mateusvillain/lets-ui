The calendar follows the WAI-ARIA APG [Date Picker Dialog](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/) pattern: a **non-modal** dialog with the days in a grid.

## Keyboard in the field

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Opens or closes the calendar. |
| `Down Arrow` or `Up Arrow` | Opens the calendar on the chosen day or on today. |
| `Alt` + `Down Arrow` | With `editable`, opens the calendar from the text box. |
| `0` to `9` | With `editable`, types the date. The mask adds the separators. |
| `Esc` | With the calendar open, closes it without changes. |

## Keyboard in the calendar

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Arrows` | Previous or next day; same column in the previous or next week. |
| `Home` / `End` | Start or end of the week. |
| `Page Up` / `Page Down` | Previous or next month. |
| `Shift` + `Page Up` / `Page Down` | Previous or next year. |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Chooses the day and closes. With `range`, the first marks one end and the second, the other. |
| `Tab` | Moves through the header buttons and the grid. Leaving closes it. |
| `Esc` | Closes without changes. With `range`, discards the incomplete selection. |

In the month and year grids, the arrows, `Home`, `End` and `Page Up` / `Page Down` work the same way. `Enter` chooses and returns to the days, and `Esc` returns to the days without changes.

## Focus

- On opening, focus goes to the chosen day or, with no value, to today.
- The grid has a single tab stop, even with two months side by side.
- On choosing or closing with `Esc`, focus returns to whatever opened the calendar.
- The calendar does not trap focus: it closes with `Tab` out or with a click outside.
- Inside a [Modal](../../content/modal/index.mdx), `Esc` closes only the calendar.

## Screen reader

- The field is named by the label and the displayed date, and announces whether the calendar is open. With `editable`, the text box is described by the format, the hint or the error. The calendar button states the chosen date: "Choose date, Jan 16, 2026".
- Each day is read in full: "Friday, January 16, 2026". Today is marked as the current date.
- The day's state is part of its name: "selected", "start of period", "in period", "end of period" or "unavailable". That way it is announced even in VoiceOver, which does not read the selection in a grid cell.
- Changing the month is announced. With `range`, after the first choice, a notice asks for the end date.
- The weekdays have their full name, even when the screen shows only the initial.
- On error, the field is announced as invalid, with the message for the case.

## Visual

- Today is bold, the selection is filled and an unavailable day has a strikethrough: none of these states depends on color alone.
- Days are 32px, and the header buttons and the month and year cells are 40px, above the WCAG 2.2 minimum of 24px.
- With `range`, the two months stack one below the other on narrow screens.

## For designers

- Define the label of every Date Picker. With no visible label, specify the `aria-label`.
- Translate all of the component's texts into the product's language: the default is English.
- Specify a message for each error.
- Do not rely on the calendar alone for distant dates: offer typing.
