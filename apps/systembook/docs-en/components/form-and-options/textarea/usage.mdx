## Size and height

- Set the initial height by the expected length: **2 to 3 lines** for a short comment, **5 or more** for a long text.
- Keep `resize` vertical (default), so the person can enlarge it without breaking the layout width.
- Use `resize="none"` only when the container requires a fixed size.

## Character counter

Turn on `maxlength` only when there is a **real** storage or display **limit**. The counter shows how much is left and avoids surprises.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Clear, visible limit">
  The "42/160" counter appears while the person types. They know how much more they can write.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Silently cutting the text">
  Blocking typing without warning, or truncating the text after submission, makes the person lose what they wrote.
</DosDonts>

## Label, hint and errors

The rules are the same as the [Input](../input/index.mdx): label always visible, hint for format and limits, and a specific error message that says how to fix it.

- In long fields, use the hint to suggest **what to write**: "Describe the problem and what you expected to happen."
- Avoid long placeholders. They disappear when the person starts typing.

## Best practices

- Do not limit the length without need. Short limits frustrate.
- Keep what the person typed if submission fails.
- Do not validate on every keystroke. Validate on leaving the field or on submit.
- For very long text, consider saving drafts automatically.
