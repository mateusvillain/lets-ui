## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` | Moves focus between the boxes. |
| `Space` | Checks or unchecks the focused box. |

## Screen reader

- The box is announced as a **checkbox**, with the label's name and its checked or unchecked state.
- On error, it is announced as invalid, and the message is read along with it. If it is required, it is also announced as such.
- When disabled, it is announced as unavailable.

## Visual

- The checked state is distinguished by more than color: a check mark appears.
- The box has a contrast of at least 3:1 against the background.
- The focus ring is visible when navigating by keyboard.

## For designers

- Always show a visible label. Only use `aria-label` when there is no room for text.
- In groups, give a title that explains the question.
- The clickable area includes the label, which helps people who have trouble with touch precision.
