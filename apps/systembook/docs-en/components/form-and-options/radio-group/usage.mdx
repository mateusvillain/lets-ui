## Group title

Every group needs a title that **asks the question**, such as "Payment method" or "How often?". Without it, the person sees loose options and screen reader users do not know what they are choosing.

## Options

- From **two to seven** options. Beyond that, a [Select](../select/index.mdx) is lighter.
- Order them logically: by the most common, by natural order (small, medium, large) or alphabetically.
- The options must be **exclusive and complete**: every possible answer fits in one of them.
- Keep the labels **parallel** in structure and length.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Parallel, exclusive options">
  "Monthly", "Quarterly" and "Yearly". Each one is clear and none overlaps.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Overlapping options">
  "Up to 10 users", "10 to 50" and "More than 10". Someone with 10 users does not know which to choose.
</DosDonts>

## Default value

Preselect an option only when there is a **reasonable, safe default**. Choices that affect money, privacy or data should be **active**: the person decides.

## Hint and error

- Use the hint to explain differences between the options or the consequences of the choice.
- When the choice is required, the error message appears below the options: "Choose a payment method." It replaces the hint.

## Position

Place the options in a **column**. In a row, only with two or three very short options. Keep consistent spacing between the options.

## Best practices

- Do not use a Radio Group for choices with immediate effect, or for navigation.
- If the person may want none, include a "None" option.
- Keep the same option order across screens.
