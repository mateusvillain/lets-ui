title: "Accessibility"
---

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Moves focus to the close button or to the action. |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Activates the focused button. |

Moving focus into the Toast **pauses the countdown**, which resumes when focus leaves.

## Screen reader

- The Toast is announced when it appears, without taking focus away from the person.
- Informative and success Toasts are announced politely (`status`). The error Toast (`danger`) is announced assertively (`alert`).
- The icon is decorative.
- The close button has an accessible name (`close-label`). Translate it into the product's language.

## Timing

- Hovering or focusing pauses the countdown.
- The `danger` Toast **never disappears on its own**: people who read slowly or use assistive technology need time to understand the error.

## For designers

- Never use a Toast as the only channel for essential information.
- Give enough time to read: about one word every 200 ms plus some extra time.
- Avoid placing it over important elements on the screen.
