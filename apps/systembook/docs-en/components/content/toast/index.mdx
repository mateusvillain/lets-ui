## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Quick feedback on an action">
  "Link copied", "Changes saved" or "Invite sent".
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Without interrupting the person">
  The message appears and disappears without pulling them away from what they are doing.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Messages to read carefully or to decide on">
  Use an [Alert](../alert/index.mdx) or a [Modal](../modal/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Errors the person needs to fix">
  Show them near the field or in an Alert.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Essential information">
  Since the Toast disappears, it can be missed.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="System notifications or history">
  Use a notification center.
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Toast anatomy: icon, title, description, action and close button](./anatomy.png)

1. **Icon**: reinforces the variant. It can be replaced.
2. **Title**: the main message.
3. **Description** (optional): supporting detail.
4. **Action** (optional): a button, such as "Undo".
5. **Close button**: appears when there is no action.

## Variants

| Variant | When to use | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Neutral information. | Disappears on its own. |
| **Success** | An action was completed. | Disappears on its own. |
| **Danger** | Something failed. | **Does not disappear on its own.** |

## Duration

By default, the Toast disappears after **5 seconds**. Hovering over it or focusing it **pauses** the countdown. With `duration="0"`, it stays until it is closed. The `danger` Toast never disappears on its own, as an accessibility decision.

## Position

It appears at the bottom center of the screen. Other positions (corners and top) are available. On small screens, it takes the full width. Several Toasts stack.
