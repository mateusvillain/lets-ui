## Content

- **Short**: one sentence or, better, a few words.
- **Text only**: no links, buttons or formatting.
- **Complementary**: the Tooltip helps, but the interface has to work without it.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Action name on an icon">
  A trash can Icon Button with the tooltip "Delete". The person confirms what the icon does.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Hide essential information">
  Putting form-filling rules or important warnings only in a Tooltip. Touch or keyboard users may never see them.
</DosDonts>

## When it appears

- On **mouse hover** and on **keyboard focus**.
- It stays open when the mouse moves from the element to the bubble.
- It disappears when the mouse or the focus leaves the element, or with `Esc`.

## Position

- Prefer `top` or `bottom`.
- Make sure the bubble **fits on the screen**, without being cut off at the edges.
- Do not cover the trigger itself or neighboring controls.

## Tooltip and mobile

On touch screens, **do not rely on the Tooltip**. Show the text directly in the interface, or use a visible label.

## Best practices

- Do not put a Tooltip on elements that already have clear text.
- Keep the same text style across all Tooltips: no final punctuation in short phrases.
- Do not use Tooltips on disabled elements without explaining the reason some other way.
