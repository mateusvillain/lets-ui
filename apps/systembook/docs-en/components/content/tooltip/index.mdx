## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Name an icon">
  Give a name to an [Icon Button](../../actionable/icon-button/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Explain an abbreviation">
  A quick clarification about a term.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Show a shortcut">
  The keyboard shortcut for an action.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Essential information">
  If the person needs it to continue, show it on the page.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Long, formatted or linked content">
  Use a Modal, a Drawer or text on the screen itself.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Touch screens">
  There is no hover, and a tap may activate the element instead of showing the hint.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Error or validation messages">
  Show them next to the field.
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Tooltip anatomy: trigger, bubble and arrow](./anatomy.png)

1. **Trigger**: the element the hint explains.
2. **Bubble**: the text box.
3. **Arrow**: points to the trigger.

## Position

The bubble appears at the `top` (default), `bottom`, `left` or `right` of the trigger. Choose the position that **does not cover** important information.

<ComponentEmbed component="Tooltip" variant="bottom" />

## Trigger

The trigger can be any focusable element. If you do not provide one, the Tooltip creates a button with the `label` text.
