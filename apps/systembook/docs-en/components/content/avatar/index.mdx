## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Authorship">
  Who wrote a comment or made a change.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Owner of an item">
  Who something belongs to, or who is a member of a team.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="The person's account">
  In the user menu, to show who is signed in.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Icons and illustrations in general">
  Use [Image](../image/index.mdx) or icons.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Opening a profile or menu">
  The Avatar is not interactive. Wrap it in a button or link, or use a [Dropdown Menu](../../actionable/dropdown-menu/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Showing the state of an item that is not a person">
  Use a [Tag](../tag/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Avatar anatomy: container, initials and status indicator](./anatomy.png)

1. **Container**: sets the size and the shape.
2. **Content**: the photo, the initials or the person icon.
3. **Status indicator** (optional): a dot in the corner, with its own shape for each state.

## Sizes

| Size | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| `lg` (default) | 40px |
| `md` | 32px |
| `sm` | 24px |

## Shapes

| Shape | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| **Circle** (default) | People. |
| **Rounded** | Organizations, projects and teams. |
| **Square** | Entities with a logo or brand. |

## Content

The Avatar picks the content in this order: **photo**, then **initials**, then the **person icon**. If the photo fails to load, the initials take its place.

## Colors

`gray` (default), `blue`, `green`, `orange`, `red` and `violet`. Color only tells one avatar from another. **It carries no meaning.**

## Status

`online`, `away`, `busy` and `offline`, each with its own color and shape, so that people who cannot tell colors apart can also tell the states apart.

<ComponentEmbed component="Avatar" variant="status" />

## Group

The Avatar Group stacks several avatars with an overlap and a ring in the surface color. It is the way to show teams and participants without taking up much space.
