## Content structure

Organize the Card in **a clear hierarchy**: a title, the supporting content and, last, the actions.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="One subject per card">
  Each Card covers one item: a project, a report, a plan. The person immediately understands what it represents.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Everything in one card">
  Mixing different subjects or stacking a lot of information in a single Card. It stops helping people scan.
</DosDonts>

## Cards in a group

- Keep **the same structure** in Cards of the same set, with title, image and actions in the same places.
- Align the Cards in a [Grid](../../layout/grid/index.mdx) with consistent spacing.
- Avoid very different heights in the same row. If the content varies, align the actions to the end.

## Actions

- Use **one main action** per Card.
- Align the actions to the end of the Card, the same way in all of them.
- If the whole Card is clickable, do not put other buttons inside it unless necessary.

## Best practices

- Give the Card an accessible name when it represents an important region, for example "Account summary".
- Use elevation sparingly: too many Cards with shadows clutter the screen.
- Do not use Cards for everything. Overuse can break the reading rhythm.
