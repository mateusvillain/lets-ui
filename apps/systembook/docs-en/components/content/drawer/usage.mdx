## Drawer or Modal?

| If... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| the person needs to **check the page** while working | Drawer |
| the content is **longer** (form, details) | Drawer |
| it is a **short confirmation or decision** | Modal |
| the task **blocks** everything else | Modal |

## Title and actions

- The title describes **the content or the task**: "Order details", "Edit profile".
- Place the main action in the **footer**, on the right, and the secondary one ("Cancel") next to it.
- Use verbs in button labels: "Save changes", instead of "OK".

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Context preserved">
  Open the Drawer from a table row. The person edits without losing their place in the list.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Drawer inside a Drawer">
  Opening a panel over another panel is disorienting. Reorganize the flow or use a page of its own.
</DosDonts>

## Closing from the backdrop

By default, clicking outside the panel **does not close it**. Enable `close-on-backdrop` only when accidental dismissal has no cost, such as in a read-only panel. In forms, keep closing explicit, so the person does not lose what they typed.

## Content

- Keep the content **focused on one task**.
- Let the body scroll, and keep the header and footer fixed.
- If there are unsaved changes, confirm before closing.

## Best practices

- Do not open the Drawer automatically when the page loads.
- Use the same size for similar Drawers.
- On small screens, the Drawer takes the full width: think about the mobile version.
