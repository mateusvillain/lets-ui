## Columns

- Show **only the essential columns**. Many columns require scrolling and make reading hard.
- Order the columns by **importance**, with the identifier (name, title) first.
- Write **short**, specific headers: "Amount" instead of "Total purchase amount in reais".

## Alignment

- Text and dates on the **left**.
- Numbers and amounts on the **right**, to make comparison easier.
- Actions at the end, aligned to the right and with a fit width.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Numbers aligned to the end">
  Values with the same number of decimal places, aligned to the right, can be compared at a glance.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Numbers aligned to the start">
  Misaligned hundreds and thousands make the magnitude hard to read.
</DosDonts>

## Status and actions

- Show the status with a [Tag](../tag/index.mdx) **with text**, not only with color.
- For row actions, use an [Icon Button](../../actionable/icon-button/index.mdx) or a [Dropdown Menu](../../actionable/dropdown-menu/index.mdx). Give it a name that mentions the row: "Actions for Ana Souza".

## Sorting

- Make sortable **only what makes sense** (name, date, amount).
- Clearly show which column is sorted and in which direction.
- The table does not reorder on its own: decide whether sorting happens in the browser or on the server.

## Lots of data

- Use [Pagination](../../navigation/pagination/index.mdx) or scrolling with a **sticky header** for long lists.
- Choose a reasonable page size, such as 10 to 50 rows.
- When the table is empty, replace it entirely with an [Empty State](../empty-state/index.mdx).

## Selection

For bulk actions, put a [Checkbox](../../form-and-options/checkbox/index.mdx) on each row and another in the header, to "select all". Show an action bar when items are selected.

## Best practices

- Keep row height consistent.
- Give the table a name, either through a visible caption or an `aria-label`.
- Avoid wrapping lines inside cells, except in running-text columns.
