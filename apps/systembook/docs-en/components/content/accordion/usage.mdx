## Write titles that describe the content

The title is what the person sees before opening. It needs to say what is inside.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Specific titles">
  "Delivery times", "How to cancel your subscription". The person knows whether it is worth opening.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Vague titles">
  "Learn more", "Details" and "Information". They force the person to open everything to find what they are looking for.
</DosDonts>

## One open or several?

- **One at a time** (default): good when the sections are alternatives and the person reads one at a time, as in an FAQ. The rule is predictable.
- **Several** (`multiple`): good when the person may need to compare or check more than one section, as in settings.

## What to leave open

Initially open the section that **most people need to read**. If almost every section needs to be read, the Accordion is not the right component.

## Number and order

- Use **three to ten** sections. With many, a list with search may help more.
- Order by importance or by the natural reading order.
- Keep titles of similar length.

## Panel content

- Prefer **short, scannable** content: short paragraphs, lists, a link or a button.
- Avoid long forms or other Accordions inside the panel.
- Avoid hiding error messages or critical actions inside a closed panel.

## Best practices

- Use the icon only if it helps identify the section, and keep the same style across all items.
- Do not use the Accordion to hide important content just to shorten the page.
- When a section opens, keep the title visible, so the person does not get lost.
