## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Frequently asked questions">
  Answers the person reads one at a time.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Settings blocks">
  Long sections grouped by subject.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Details, terms and policies">
  Long content that does not need to be fully open.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Essential information">
  If almost everyone needs to read it, keep it visible.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Navigating between different areas">
  Use [Tabs](../../navigation/tabs/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Short content">
  If it fits on the screen, there is no need to hide it.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Steps of a flow">
  Use a step indicator.
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Accordion anatomy: header, icon, title, subtitle, chevron and panel](./anatomy.png)

1. **Header**: the whole strip is clickable and works as a button.
2. **Icon** (optional): decorative, to the left of the title.
3. **Title**: names the section.
4. **Subtitle** (optional): supporting text below the title.
5. **Chevron**: rotates to indicate open or closed.
6. **Panel**: the revealed content.

## Variants

| Variant | Description | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Items separated by dividers. | Content pages, FAQs. |
| **Bordered** | The whole list inside a rounded border. | Contexts that call for a frame, such as cards. |
| **Highlighted** | Outlines the open item. | When it matters to locate the active item. |

## Behavior

- By default, **one item open at a time**: opening one closes the previous one.
- With `multiple`, the person can open several at the same time.
- Disabled items do not toggle and leave the focus order.
