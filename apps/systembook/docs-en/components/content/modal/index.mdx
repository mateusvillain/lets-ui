## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Important or destructive action">
  The person confirms before continuing.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Short, focused task">
  Renaming an item or inviting someone.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Information that blocks the flow">
  Something the person needs to read before moving on.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Messages that do not require a decision">
  Use [Alert](../alert/index.mdx) or [Toast](../toast/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Long content or multi-step flows">
  Use a dedicated page or a [Drawer](../drawer/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Referring to the page while working">
  Use a Drawer.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Tips and contextual help">
  Use a [Tooltip](../tooltip/index.mdx).
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Anatomy of the open Modal: backdrop, window, header, body and footer](./anatomy.png)

1. **Backdrop**: dims the page behind.
2. **Window**: the centered surface.
3. **Header**: title and close button.
4. **Body**: the content.
5. **Footer** (optional): action buttons.

## Sizes

| Size | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| `sm` | Short confirmations. |
| `md` (default) | Most cases. |
| `lg` | Content with more information, such as small forms. |

## Behavior

- Opens in the center of the screen, with a backdrop.
- Focus moves into the window, and the page behind becomes inaccessible.
- Closes with the close button, with `Esc` and with the action buttons.
- On close, focus returns to the element that opened the Modal.

## Without actions

If the Modal is only informative, omit the footer. The close button is enough.
