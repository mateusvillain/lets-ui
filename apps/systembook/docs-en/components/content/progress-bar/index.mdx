## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Long waits">
  When the task takes long enough that the person wants to know how much is left, such as uploading files or importing data.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Progress measured in any unit">
  Percentage, GB, steps: the bar fills in proportion to the value over the maximum.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Quota usage">
  Storage or a plan limit, with `caution` when it is close to the end.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Area that loads with a known shape">
  Use a skeleton, which shows the shape of the content that is coming.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Named steps">
  For the steps of a flow, such as sign-up or checkout, use a step indicator.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Short waits">
  A bar that appears and disappears in less than a second just flickers on the screen.
</DosDonts>

## Anatomy

![Progress Bar anatomy: label, value, track, fill and caption](./anatomy.png)

1. **Label**: names the task.
2. **Value**: the progress as a percentage, or in the `value-text` text.
3. **Track**: the whole task.
4. **Fill**: the part already done.
5. **Caption** (optional): the progress and the result, such as "8 of 20 files" or "Upload complete".

## Determinate and indeterminate

With `value`, the bar fills in proportion to `value` over `max`. Without `value`, it is **indeterminate**: a segment runs along the track while there is no way to measure progress. As soon as the measurement exists, the bar switches to determinate mode.

## Variants

| Variant | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` (default) | The task is in progress. |
| `success` | The task finished well. |
| `caution` | The task continues, but needs attention, such as an almost full quota. |
| `danger` | The task failed or went over the limit. |

<ComponentEmbed component="ProgressBar" variant="success" />

<ComponentEmbed component="ProgressBar" variant="danger" />

Every `success`, `caution` or `danger` bar carries the outcome in the caption. Color alone does not convey the state to people who cannot distinguish it, nor to the screen reader.

## Label placement

In `top` (default), the label and the value sit above the track. In `inline`, the label, track and value sit on the same line, which saves height in lists and tables. The caption is always below.

## Sizes

| Size | Track thickness |
| --- | --- |
| `sm` | 4px |
| `md` (default) | 8px |
| `lg` | 12px |

Only the track changes: the text stays the same.
