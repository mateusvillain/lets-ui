## Screen reader

- The alternative text (`alt`) is what the screen reader announces. Describe what the image **communicates**.
- Decorative images use an empty `alt` and are left out of the reading.
- With `caption`, the image and the text form a figure with a caption, and the screen reader recognizes the relationship.

## Visual

- Do not rely on an image alone to convey important information: repeat it in the text.
- Images with text need contrast and a text alternative.
- The fallback color must not hurt the contrast of any text that appears over the image.

## For designers

- Write the `alt` along with the image, during design.
- Consider people on slow connections: the placeholder and the fallback keep the page understandable.
- Avoid images that flash or move without control.
