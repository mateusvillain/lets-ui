title: "Accessibility"
order: 3
---

Stack is layout only and **adds no semantics**. The points to watch are in the **content** you arrange with it.

- **Reading order**: Stack keeps the source order, which is the keyboard and screen reader order. The visual order must be the same.
- **Zoom and large text**: the column adapts well to 200% zoom. Avoid fixed heights.
- **Spacing**: space also helps people who have trouble focusing, because it separates groups.
