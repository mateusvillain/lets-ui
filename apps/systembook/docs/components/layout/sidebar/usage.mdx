## Side area at the start or the end

- **Start** for navigation and filters, which the person uses before reading the content.
- **End** for supporting information: summary, metadata, help.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Side area for support and navigation">
  Filters on the left and results on the right. The main content takes the remaining space.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Side area competing with the content">
  A side area that is too wide or packed with information. The main content gets squeezed.
</DosDonts>

## Width

Choose `side-width` based on the **content of the side area**: 200 to 320px covers most cases. Make sure the longest text fits without wrapping too much.

## Behavior on small screens

When the content does not have its minimum space (`content-min-width`), the areas **stack**. Think about the order: on mobile, the side area comes **before** the content (if `side="start"`). If the side area is long, consider hiding it behind a button.

## Best practices

- Give long side areas independent scrolling with a [Scroll Area](../scroll-area/index.mdx).
- Keep the side area consistent across the product's pages.
- In navigation side areas, highlight the current page.
