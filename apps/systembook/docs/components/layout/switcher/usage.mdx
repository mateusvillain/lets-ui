## Items of equal weight

Switcher distributes items in **equal parts**. Use it when all of them have the same importance and roughly the same width.

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Equivalent cards">
  Three pricing plans that share the width and stack together on small screens.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Items of different weights">
  Main content with a smaller side area is not a case for Switcher. Use Sidebar.
</DosDonts>

## Set the switch point

`threshold` is the width at which the change happens. Choose it based on the **minimum comfortable size** of each item: if each card needs about 240px, set `threshold` to around three times that when you want three columns.

## Item limit

With `limit`, items stack once they go past a certain number. This helps avoid too many cramped columns. With three items and `limit="3"`, a fourth item makes all of them stack.

## Best practices

- Keep the content of the items **similarly structured**.
- Test Switcher in containers of varying widths, such as side areas and panels.
- Combine it with a [Stack](../stack/index.mdx) inside each item to arrange the content.
