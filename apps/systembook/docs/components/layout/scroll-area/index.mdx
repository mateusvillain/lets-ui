## Best practices

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Long lists in panels">
  Items that do not fit in the height of a panel or a side area.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Code snippets">
  Long lines that scroll without breaking the layout.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="do" title="Carousels">
  Content that scrolls within a fixed space.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="The whole page">
  Let the browser's scrolling handle it.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Avoiding horizontal scrolling in tables">
  [Table](../../content/table/index.mdx) already handles its own scrolling.
</DosDonts>

<DosDonts variant="dont" title="Hiding important content">
  Content that requires scrolling can go unnoticed.
</DosDonts>

## Options

| Option | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| `orientation` | Scroll axis: vertical (default) or horizontal. |
| `scrollbar-visibility` | When the scrollbar appears: `auto`, `always`, `hover` or `hidden`. |
| `fade` | Gradient at the edges, in the background color, that signals hidden content. |
| `scroll-shadow` | Shadow at the edges, which works on any background. |
| `snap` | Scrolling "snaps" to the items, good for carousels. |
| `overscroll` | Prevents scrolling from passing to the page when it reaches the limit. |
| `height`, `max-height`, `max-width` | The size of the region. |

## Scroll indicators

The indicators show that there is **more content** past the visible edge. They appear and disappear on their own as you scroll.
