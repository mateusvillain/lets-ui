## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Moves focus to the button. |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Activates the button. |

Disabled buttons leave the tab order. While loading, focus stays where it was.

## Screen reader

- The accessible name comes from the label. If the label is too short to give context (like "Edit" in a list), complement it with `aria-label`: "Edit Ana Lima's profile".
- While loading, the button is announced as busy (`aria-busy`).
- A button with `href` is announced as a **link**, because that is what it is.

## Color and contrast

- The system targets WCAG 2.2 AA: text with a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against the background. Every variant passes in both themes.
- On hover and pressed, the background moves away from the label color, so contrast **increases** in those states instead of dropping: the filled variants darken in the light theme and lighten in the dark one, and `neutral` does the opposite.
- Meaning never depends on color alone: the label of the `danger` button still says "Delete".

## Focus

The focus ring appears when navigating with the keyboard and must have a minimum contrast of 3:1 against the background. Never remove the focus outline.

## For designers

- Write labels that make sense out of context, because screen readers list every button on the page.
- Keep the touch target at least 24 × 24px, even at size `md`.
- Do not use buttons just to navigate: use links.
