## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` | Moves focus to the Switch. |
| `Space` | Turns it on or off. |

## Screen reader

- The control is announced as a **switch**, with the label's name and its state (on or off).
- When disabled, it is announced as unavailable.
- On error, it is announced as invalid, and the message is read along with it.

## Visual

- The state is communicated by the **position** of the thumb, in addition to color.
- The track and the thumb have a minimum contrast of 3:1 against the background.
- The focus ring is visible when navigating by keyboard.

## For designers

- Always show a visible label, in the same place on every Switch.
- Do not use color alone to indicate on and off.
- Keep the touch area comfortable, including the label.
