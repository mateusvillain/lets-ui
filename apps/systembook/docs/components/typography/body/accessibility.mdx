## Screen reader

The Body renders a paragraph (`p`), and the text is read in content order.

## Visual

- The text needs a minimum contrast of **4.5:1** against the background. Large text accepts 3:1.
- The text must be able to **zoom up to 200%** without losing content. That is why the sizes use relative units.
- Do not use color alone to highlight important information.

## For designers

- Avoid justified text and very long lines.
- Use the `sm` size with care: small text is hard to read for many people.
- Set the content **language** on the page, so the screen reader pronounces it correctly.
