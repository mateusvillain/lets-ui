## Keyboard

Tag is **not interactive**: it does not receive focus or respond to the keyboard.

## Screen reader

- The Tag text is read as part of the surrounding content. That is why the label must make sense together with the item: "Order 1042, Paid".
- Do not use color alone to convey the status. The text is essential.

## Visual

- The text has a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against the background, in every variant.
- Different variants do not depend on hue alone: the text indicates the meaning.

## For designers

- Choose labels that work outside the visual context.
- Do not use colors that look the same to people with color blindness for opposite meanings, such as `success` and `danger`, without text.
