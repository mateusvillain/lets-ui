## Keyboard

The Card itself does not receive focus. Focus goes to the **interactive elements inside it**, such as links and buttons, in content order.

## Screen reader

- The Card has an accessible name (`aria-label`, default "Card"). Replace it with a name that describes the content ("Account summary") when it represents an important region.
- The buttons in the `actions` slot sit in a group (`role="group"`) with its own name, set by `actions-label` (default "Card actions"). Translate it into the page's language: on a Portuguese page, "Ações do card".
- The internal structure depends on the content you put in. Use real headings (`h2`, `h3`) for the Card title, respecting the page hierarchy.

## For designers

- Make sure the text inside the Card has a 4.5:1 contrast against the surface.
- If the whole Card is clickable, the target needs a link with descriptive text, not just "Learn more".
- Do not rely on color or shadow alone to separate Cards: spacing and the border help.
