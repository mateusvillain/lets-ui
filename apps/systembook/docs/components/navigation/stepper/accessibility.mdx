## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Moves through the steps. |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Goes to the focused step. |

In a `linear` stepper, the steps ahead of the current one stay in the focus order, with `aria-disabled="true"`, and do not respond.

## Screen reader

- The steps form a list (`role="list"` and `role="listitem"`), named by `label`, such as "Checkout".
- Each step is a native button. The current one has `aria-current="step"`.
- The name of each step starts with the position and the total ("Step 2 of 4, Address, Shipping"). The visible number has `aria-hidden="true"`, so it is not read twice.
- Completed steps carry the `completed-label` text, and loading steps the `loading-label` text and `aria-busy="true"`: the state is not conveyed by color alone.
- After every change, by click or by code, a `role="status"` region announces the new step ("Step 3 of 4, Payment"). A canceled change is not announced.
- The connectors are decorative (`aria-hidden="true"`).

## Visual

- A horizontal stepper that does not fit its container switches to vertical instead of scrolling sideways (WCAG 1.4.10, Reflow).
- The state shown by the color of the indicator is also in the accessible name, through `completed-label` and `loading-label`.

## For designers

- Translate the accessible texts (`label`, `completed-label`, `loading-label` and `step-label`) into the product language.
- When the step changes the page content, plan the new page title (for example "Payment, step 3 of 4, Checkout") and where the focus goes.
- In a stepper with numbers only, give it a descriptive `label`.
