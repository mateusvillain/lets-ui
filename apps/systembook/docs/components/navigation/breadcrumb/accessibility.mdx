## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Moves through the links of the path, in order. |
| `Enter` | Opens the focused link. |

## Screen reader

- The Breadcrumb is a navigation landmark with a name, such as "Breadcrumb" or "Navigation trail". Give it a name in the product's language with `aria-label`.
- The items form an ordered list, and the screen reader announces the number of levels.
- The current item is marked with `aria-current="page"`.

## For designers

- The separator is decorative. Do not rely on the separator alone to communicate the relationship between levels.
- Ensure a contrast of at least 4.5:1 on the links and a comfortable touch target on small screens.
