## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Moves through the pagination buttons. |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Goes to the chosen page. |

After navigating, focus stays on the control that was used. If it becomes disabled or disappears (like "Next" on the last page), focus moves to the current page.

## Screen reader

- The pagination is a navigation landmark with a name, set by `label`.
- The current page is announced as current (`aria-current="page"`).
- Each page has a name like "Page 3", and the previous and next buttons have their own names.
- After each change, an invisible region announces "Page 3 of 20".
- The ellipses are decorative and stay out of the accessibility tree.

## For designers

- Translate the accessible texts (`previous-label`, `next-label`, `page-label` and `status-label`) into the product's language.
- Make sure the current page stands out through more than color.
- Keep a comfortable touch target: the buttons are small and close together.
