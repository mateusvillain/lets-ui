## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Tab` | Enters the tab list, on the active tab. A second press moves to the panel. |
| `Right Arrow` | Activates the next enabled tab. |
| `Left Arrow` | Activates the previous enabled tab. |
| `Home` | Activates the first enabled tab. |
| `End` | Activates the last enabled tab. |

Disabled tabs are skipped.

## Screen reader

- The list is announced as `tablist`, each tab as `tab` with its selected state, and the content as `tabpanel`, linked to its tab.
- Only the active tab is in the tab order. The arrows move between the others.
- The inactive panel is hidden from assistive technology.

## For designers

- Do not indicate the active tab with color alone: also use the indicator (line or segment).
- Ensure a contrast of 3:1 on the indicator and 4.5:1 on the text.
- Keep labels short so they do not wrap onto two lines.
